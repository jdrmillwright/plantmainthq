eMaint

Fluke Reliability's highly configurable enterprise CMMS and condition monitoring platform.

4.5 / 5.0
1120+ Verified Reviews

eMaint, a Fluke Reliability company, is an enterprise-grade, deeply configurable Computerized Maintenance Management System and EAM suite. Engineered to handle complex continuous process industries and heavily regulated facilities, eMaint integrates directly with Fluke condition monitoring sensors and diagnostic tools. It provides organizations with extensive workflow customization, interactive plant image mapping, robust MRO inventory management, and stringent regulatory audit compliance.

✓ What Plant Teams Love (Strengths)

  • ✓Fluke sensor ecosystem: Direct plug-and-play integration with Fluke 3561 vibration sensors, thermal imaging systems, and power quality loggers for real-time condition triggers.
  • ✓Infinite configurability: System administrators can customize tables, custom fields, data validation rules, and approval routings without writing custom software code.
  • ✓Interactive image mapping: Technicians can click directly on plant schematics, CAD floorplans, or P&ID diagrams to inspect equipment health and launch work orders.
  • ✓Exemplary regulatory compliance: Strict audit logs, electronic signatures, and historical records engineered for strict FDA, EPA, and OSHA regulatory inspections.
  • ✓Industrial multi-warehouse logistics: Robust multi-site inventory transfers, repairable spares tracking, vendor warranty monitoring, and automated purchasing.

⚠ Limitations & Trade-offs

  • ⚠Extended implementation timeline (6 to 12 weeks) requiring dedicated project management, IT coordination, and administrator training.
  • ⚠Administrative desktop console exhibits legacy styling and high configuration density that can overwhelm novice users.
  • ⚠Higher initial cost of entry and consulting investment compared to plug-and-play SaaS alternatives.

PlantMaintHQ Editorial Verdict

eMaint by Fluke Reliability is a powerhouse CMMS built for high-consequence industrial facilities and plants with serious predictive maintenance, condition monitoring, or regulatory compliance mandates.

IDEAL FIT (WHO IT'S FOR)

Mid-to-large process manufacturing plants, pharmaceutical facilities, and utilities that require deep customization, Fluke sensor integration, and bulletproof compliance auditing.

CAUTION (WHO SHOULD AVOID)

Light commercial operations or small maintenance crews seeking an out-of-the-box system that can be deployed without formal training.
