UpKeep

Asset Operations Management platform connecting maintenance, reliability, and operations data.

4.7 / 5.0
1940+ Verified Reviews

UpKeep is a modern, cloud-based Asset Operations Management (AOM) platform and CMMS designed to unite maintenance technicians, plant reliability engineers, and operations managers under a single source of truth. UpKeep combines work order dispatch, preventive maintenance scheduling, and MRO parts tracking with native Edge IoT condition monitoring sensors, providing plant teams with actionable insights into machine health, downtime costs, and workforce productivity.

✓ What Plant Teams Love (Strengths)

  • ✓Holistic Asset Operations Management: Unifies frontline task execution with executive reliability reporting and asset financial depreciation.
  • ✓Integrated IoT hardware: Native UpKeep Edge wireless vibration and temperature sensors allow plants to launch condition-based predictive maintenance programs without third-party gateways.
  • ✓Comprehensive parts inventory: Multi-stockroom tracking with automated reorder thresholds, barcode bin scanning, and PO approval routing.
  • ✓Field-proven mobile app: Technicians can quickly log hours, allocate consumed spare parts to assets, and close out work orders from their smartphones.
  • ✓Rich ecosystem: Extensive pre-built integrations with ERPs, accounting packages, and business automation platforms via Zapier and open REST APIs.

⚠ Limitations & Trade-offs

  • ⚠Licensing costs scale rapidly: Per-technician subscription pricing can become substantial for large plant crews requiring full system access.
  • ⚠Advanced workflow automation, custom roles, and API access are gated behind premium tiers ($75+/user/mo).
  • ⚠Reporting console query speeds can exhibit latency when generating complex historical reports over high work-order volumes.

PlantMaintHQ Editorial Verdict

UpKeep is a highly mature CMMS platform that bridges the gap between field maintenance tasks and corporate asset performance management, made stronger by its native wireless IoT ecosystem.

IDEAL FIT (WHO IT'S FOR)

Industrial facilities seeking a balance of modern mobile convenience, structured spare parts inventory control, and accessible condition-based sensor integration.

CAUTION (WHO SHOULD AVOID)

Budget-constrained small operations that only need a basic digital work order dispatch board without needing inventory control or IoT metrics.
