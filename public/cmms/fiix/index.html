Fiix

AI-powered CMMS and enterprise reliability platform backed by Rockwell Automation.

4.6 / 5.0
1280+ Verified Reviews

Fiix, a Rockwell Automation company, is an enterprise-grade, cloud-based CMMS that combines asset management, work order optimization, and parts inventory control with advanced artificial intelligence. Leveraging its Fiix Foresight AI analytics engine and native connectivity to industrial automation networks, Fiix helps manufacturing operations transition from reactive emergency repairs to predictive, condition-based maintenance programs. It is designed to scale across multi-facility enterprises requiring strict compliance auditing and deep equipment telemetry integration.

✓ What Plant Teams Love (Strengths)

  • ✓Rockwell Automation synergy: Seamless telemetry pipeline with FactoryTalk, Allen-Bradley PLCs, and SCADA infrastructure for real-time fault code integration.
  • ✓Predictive AI insights: Fiix Foresight machine learning models analyze historical work orders to identify hidden failure triggers before catastrophic machine breakdown occurs.
  • ✓Enterprise multi-site standardization: Central corporate engineering can benchmark mean time between failures (MTBF), wrench time, and PM compliance across multiple plants.
  • ✓Deep inventory architecture: Multi-location stockrooms, automated vendor replenishment, cycle counting, and detailed part obsolescence tracking.
  • ✓Rigorous audit compliance: Complete historical audit trails, electronic signature captures, and regulatory reporting suitable for ISO, OSHA, and FDA inspections.

⚠ Limitations & Trade-offs

  • ⚠Longer implementation cycle (4 to 8 weeks) requiring rigorous data cleanup, asset taxonomy mapping, and staff training.
  • ⚠The mobile application prioritizes industrial utility over sleek consumer aesthetics, carrying a slight learning curve for novice mechanics.
  • ⚠Unlocking predictive AI value requires a consistent baseline of clean historical maintenance records.

PlantMaintHQ Editorial Verdict

Fiix is the premier CMMS choice for mature industrial manufacturing plants—particularly those running Rockwell Automation hardware—that want to move past basic work order dispatching into true predictive reliability engineering.

IDEAL FIT (WHO IT'S FOR)

Mid-to-large manufacturing plants, automotive plants, and heavy industrial facilities with dedicated reliability personnel seeking deep automation integration.

CAUTION (WHO SHOULD AVOID)

Small repair operations, commercial facilities, or organizations without the bandwidth to conduct structured asset data migrations.
