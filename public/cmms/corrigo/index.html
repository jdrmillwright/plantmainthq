System Overview

Corrigo (a JLL company) is an enterprise facilities maintenance management platform managing billions of square feet worldwide. It delivers automated work order triage, contractor scorecards, asset maintenance lifecycles, and advanced spend analytics across global corporate real estate.

💡 Editor's Verdict

Corrigo Enterprise is a comprehensive maintenance solution with strong usability (7.9/10). Its combination of AI-Powered Work Order Routing, Subcontractor Performance Scorecards, Preventive Maintenance Automation makes it a top consideration for modernizing plant operations.

Target Recommendation: Facilities & Property, Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals, Packaging & Logistics maintenance and reliability teams.
